## 1. Attract

- Contact 5 appropriate prospects per week.
- Post 3 approved haircut results per week.
- Clearly state that the apprentice is working under supervision.
- Promote one haircut family at a time instead of claiming mastery.
- Use only shop-approved model/apprentice pricing.
- Never promise a result the apprentice has not demonstrated.

### Model invitation script

> Hi, this is [your name] from Artisan Barber. I’m completing my supervised apprenticeship under Charlie McCoy and am currently building my work in classic tapers, clipper-and-scissor cuts, and textured styles. I’m looking for a few models for supervised appointments. The service may take a little longer while I complete the training process. Would you be interested in one of my available appointments?

## 2. Convert

### Ask

> What do you like about your current haircut?
>
> What would you change about it?
>
> How do you normally style your hair?
>
> How much time do you want to spend styling it?
>
> Do you prefer a matte, natural, or polished finish?

### Repeat the plan

> Based on what you told me, I recommend keeping more weight here, tapering this area, and leaving enough length on top for movement. I’ll finish it with a natural product rather than a high-shine product. Does that match what you have in mind?

## 3. Retain

Before the client leaves:

- Front/profile/back photos
- One styling technique
- One product recommendation
- Next maintenance appointment
- Record formula/preferences
- Follow up within 24 hours

### Rebooking script

> To keep this shape looking intentional, I recommend your next appointment in about three to four weeks. Let’s reserve it now so you have the time that works best for you.

## 4. Referrals

### Referral script

> I’m building my clientele here at Artisan. When someone compliments your haircut, I would appreciate you sharing my booking information with them.

The referral ask is based on service quality, not pressure or exaggerated promises.

## Weekly Apprentice Scorecard

- Consultation: 15
- Sectioning / tool control / safe technique: 15
- Shape / proportion / silhouette: 20
- Blend / detailing / hairline / finish: 20
- Product selection / application: 10
- Professionalism / cleanliness / client comfort: 10
- Timing / home-care / rebooking: 10

**Total:** 100. **Passing:** 80.

A serious safety, sanitation, or conduct issue can fail the service regardless of numerical score.

## Client and Service Tracker

Record each service with:

- Date
- Client
- Haircut
- Product used
- Service time
- Photo permission
- Rebooked
- Follow-up sent
- Instructor notes

Charlie reviews it weekly to identify haircut repetition, product knowledge, where time is being lost, returning clients, consultation/retention patterns, and portfolio development.
