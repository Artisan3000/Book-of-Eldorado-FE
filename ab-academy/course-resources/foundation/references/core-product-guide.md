The shop also carries skin, body, fragrance, and home products, but your first product-learning phase should concentrate on products that directly support haircuts, styling, beard care, and home maintenance.

## All-Purpose Pomade

**What to know:** Strong, long-lasting hold; low-shine finish; water-based; apply a dime-sized amount to damp or dry hair.

**Best haircut pairings:** Side parts, slick backs, classic clipper-and-scissor cuts, polished pompadours

**Client-friendly explanation:** “This gives you strong control without making the hair look excessively shiny.”

## Texturizing Paste

**What to know:** Flexible hold, natural finish, volume, movement, and separation; suitable for short texture or longer loose styles.

**Best haircut pairings:** French crops, textured tops, medium scissor cuts, loose waves

**Client-friendly explanation:** “This keeps the style movable and natural while giving the hair separation.”

## Clay Pomade

**What to know:** Strong hold, matte finish, and added volume; designed especially for shorter, defined styles.

**Best haircut pairings:** Skin fades with textured tops, crops, short pompadours, thick short hair

**Client-friendly explanation:** “This is the better choice when you want strong hold with almost no shine.”

## 40 Proof Sea Salt Spray

**What to know:** Light-to-medium hold and textured finish; apply from the roots on damp or towel-dried hair.

**Best haircut pairings:** Beach cuts, textured scissor cuts, waves, pompadours, pre-styling

**Client-friendly explanation:** “This helps create lift and texture before we finish the style.”

## 30 Proof Styling Cream

**What to know:** Light hold, natural sheen, and weightless control; functions as a pre-styler, daily styler, or light beard tamer.

**Best haircut pairings:** Scissor cuts, softer tapers, medium-length hair, light beard control

**Client-friendly explanation:** “This keeps the hair controlled but soft and natural rather than stiff.”

## Advanced Volumizing Foam

**What to know:** Flexible hold and added fullness; intended to create volume without heavy residue or crunch.

**Best haircut pairings:** Fine hair, textured pompadours, medium-length cuts, blow-dried styles

**Client-friendly explanation:** “This helps the hair look fuller before we add the finishing product.”

## Hair Pomade

**What to know:** Firm hold, volume, shape, separation, flyaway control, and dry-end smoothing; may be used on wet or dry hair.

**Best haircut pairings:** Classic shapes, curls, side parts, slick styles, next-day refreshes

**Client-friendly explanation:** “This gives control and definition while still helping smooth the ends.”

## Equilibrium Shampoo

**What to know:** Gentle, balancing cleanser; massage into wet hair with attention to the scalp, rinse, and follow with conditioner.

**Best haircut pairings:** General home-care recommendation before any styled haircut

**Client-friendly explanation:** “This is the cleansing step; the conditioner then restores manageability.”

## Everyday Conditioner

**What to know:** Lightweight hydration and manageability without excessive weight; concentrate on mid-lengths and ends.

**Best haircut pairings:** Medium and long scissor cuts, dry-feeling hair, regular wash routines

**Client-friendly explanation:** “This helps keep the hair manageable so the haircut styles more easily at home.”

## Beard Balm

**What to know:** Softens, shapes, and hydrates the beard and the skin beneath it.

**Best haircut pairings:** Classic, tapered, mid-length, and scissor-finished beards

**Client-friendly explanation:** “This helps soften the beard and keeps the finished shape controlled.”
