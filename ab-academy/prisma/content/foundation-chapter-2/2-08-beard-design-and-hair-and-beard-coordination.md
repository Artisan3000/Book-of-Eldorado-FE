## Lesson objective

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to shape a beard according to the client’s density, face, and desired length, coordinate the beard with the haircut, and recommend grooming products without making claims the products are not designed to support.

## The beard is part of the silhouette

A beard trim is not just taking the beard shorter.

You are balancing length, cleaning the outline, shaping the face, and deciding how the beard connects to the haircut.

This lesson covers classic, traditional, mid-length, all-scissor, tapered, and skin-faded beard trims, plus the full hair-and-beard combination.

The best combination services feel coordinated from the sideburn through the beard line. Haircut, beard, and outline should read as one decision.

## Confirm the length before you remove it

Beards carry history. Clients often have more attachment to that length than they realize until it is gone.

Confirm the desired length clearly. Evaluate density and patchiness. Look at the cheek and neck lines. Consider what the beard is doing to the face shape before you start changing it.

And before removing additional length during the service, show the client the shape.

It is much easier to take more off than to explain why you took too much.

## Keep the outline believable

A clean beard line should look intentional without looking disconnected from the person wearing it.

Work on cheek and neck guidelines, connect the sideburns into the haircut, use scissor detailing where needed, and be careful about creating an outline that feels too artificial for the client’s density or growth.

The goal is polish, not a line that only works from one camera angle.

## Instructor standard

Charlie or another supervising professional will demonstrate the approved beard-length control, guideline placement, tapering, sideburn connection, and scissor-detailing process. Beard design changes with density and face shape, so the sequence must stay responsive to the client.

## Product focus: cosmetic care, not medical promises

Beard Balm is used for cosmetic softening, shaping, and hydration.

30 Proof Styling Cream can be used sparingly when loose beard hairs need light control.

Keep the language honest. Do not promise hair growth. Do not claim a grooming product treats a skin condition. Do not guarantee medical results.

You are recommending grooming products for appearance, control, and care. Stay inside that lane.

## Client-building application: recommend the complete service ethically

Hair and beard do not always grow on the same schedule.

When the client would genuinely benefit from coordinating both, explain what you see and offer the combination service without turning it into pressure.

A simple version: your haircut and beard are growing on different schedules. Today I can coordinate the sideburn transition and beard outline so the whole look feels intentional. If you want, I can explain the combination service.

That is a recommendation. The client still decides.
