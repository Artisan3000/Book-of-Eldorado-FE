Artisan Academy — Chapter 2 Workbook

Haircut Development & Product Knowledge

This workbook accompanies Chapter 2 of Artisan Academy. Use the website lessons for instruction, then use these pages to plan the work, document supervised services, reflect on feedback, track client-building activity, and prepare for the final capstone.

# How to use this workbook

Bring this workbook into the work itself. Fill out the planning sections before a service when the lesson asks you to. Complete the service notes while the details are still fresh. Use the scorecard and instructor feedback to decide what needs another repetition.

The goal is not to complete pages for the sake of completing pages. The goal is to create a record of how your judgment, technique, timing, product knowledge, and client book are developing across the chapter.

# Chapter 2 targets

- By the end of Chapter 2, you are working toward:
- 30 documented supervised services across the chapter.
- Consistent execution across the foundational haircut families.
- Professional consultations that turn the client’s goal into a clear haircut plan.
- Product recommendations based on hold, finish, length, density, texture, and routine.
- A clear at-home styling explanation for the client.
- A portfolio of at least 15 approved looks with permission.
- A starter clientele of at least 12 returning or rebooking clients.
- Progress toward a 45-minute haircut target where appropriate without sacrificing quality.

# Reference — Core Product Guide

## All-Purpose Pomade

What to know: Strong, long-lasting hold; low-shine finish; water-based; about a dime-sized amount on damp or dry hair.

Best pairings: Side parts, slick backs, classic clipper-and-scissor cuts, polished pompadours.

Client-friendly explanation: Strong control without excessive shine.

## Texturizing Paste

What to know: Flexible hold, natural finish, volume, movement, and separation.

Best pairings: French crops, textured tops, medium scissor cuts, loose waves.

Client-friendly explanation: Keeps the style movable and natural while adding separation.

## Clay Pomade

What to know: Strong hold, matte finish, and added volume; especially useful on shorter, defined styles.

Best pairings: Skin fades with textured tops, crops, short pompadours, thick short hair.

Client-friendly explanation: Strong hold with almost no shine.

## 40 Proof Sea Salt Spray

What to know: Light-to-medium hold and textured finish; apply from the roots on damp or towel-dried hair.

Best pairings: Beach cuts, textured scissor cuts, waves, pompadours, pre-styling.

Client-friendly explanation: Creates lift and texture before the final styling step.

## 30 Proof Styling Cream

What to know: Light hold, natural sheen, and weightless control; can work as a pre-styler, daily styler, or light beard tamer.

Best pairings: Scissor cuts, softer tapers, medium-length hair, light beard control.

Client-friendly explanation: Keeps the hair controlled but soft and natural rather than stiff.

## Advanced Volumizing Foam

What to know: Flexible hold and added fullness without aiming for heavy residue or crunch.

Best pairings: Fine hair, textured pompadours, medium-length cuts, blow-dried styles.

Client-friendly explanation: Helps the hair look fuller before the finishing product.

## Hair Pomade

What to know: Firm hold, volume, shape, separation, flyaway control, and dry-end smoothing; usable on wet or dry hair.

Best pairings: Classic shapes, curls, side parts, slick styles, next-day refreshes.

Client-friendly explanation: Gives control and definition while helping smooth the ends.

## Equilibrium Shampoo

What to know: Gentle balancing cleanser. Massage into wet hair with attention to the scalp, rinse, then follow with conditioner.

Best pairings: General home-care recommendation before styled haircuts.

Client-friendly explanation: The cleansing step before conditioner restores manageability.

## Everyday Conditioner

What to know: Lightweight hydration and manageability; concentrate on mid-lengths and ends and rinse after two to three minutes.

Best pairings: Medium and long scissor cuts, dry-feeling hair, regular wash routines.

Client-friendly explanation: Helps keep the hair manageable so the haircut styles more easily at home.

## Beard Balm

What to know: Cosmetic softening, shaping, and hydration for the beard and skin beneath it.

Best pairings: Classic, tapered, mid-length, and scissor-finished beards.

Client-friendly explanation: Helps soften the beard and keep the finished shape controlled.
